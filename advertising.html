About advertising

Advertising copy has one job: to make the next step feel obvious. That means one idea per piece, and words chosen for the person reading them rather than the brand writing them.

Here is the shape of one product page, marked up the way our editors read it.

Product page  ·  Cold brew kit

Cold brew in eleven minutes. No cafe needed.

the benefit, in the headline

Pour, press, wait eleven minutes. The kit does the slow part, so your first cup is ready before the school run.

what it does, before what it is

Makes four cups at a time. Rinses clean in under a minute. Fits in the fridge door.

three specifics a buyer can picture

One 250g bag makes a week of coffee for two.

a reason to believe, stated plainly

AED 149, delivered across Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

price and the action, nothing in the way

What to put in your brief

Three things that make the first draft land closer. Each one takes a minute in the brief form.

Lead with the offer

What you sell, the price if it matters and the one thing that makes it different. That is where the copy starts.

Say where it runs

A landing page, a product grid, a billboard. Space and placement decide how many words a line can carry there.

Must have

Give us the action

Buy, book, sign up or call. One clear action per piece, so the copy moves every reader the same way.
